Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026 · Applies to the «منبه صحصح» iPhone app, version 1.0 (shown on the Home Screen as «صحصح»). The Arabic version is the primary one; if the two differ, the Arabic governs.

Who we are

The app is published by SPEACE TECH, Commercial Registration No. 7054590067, Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Privacy contact: founder@speacetech.com

In short

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • Your alarms, preferences and history are stored only on your phone. We do not collect them, the app does not send them to any server, and we have no server that could receive them.
  • The app is free. It shows two Google banner ads, one at the top of the screen and one at the bottom, on three screens: Alarms, History and Settings; and after you end an alarm inside the app a full-screen ad may appear, which you can close. To show and measure ads, Google processes some device data, listed below.
  • Personalised ads appear only if you allow tracking. You can change your mind at any time, as easily as you agreed.
  • No ad ever appears on the alarm screen or inside a wake-up mission.

1. Data that stays on your device and that we do not collect

The app keeps this data on your iPhone only, in the app's on-device storage:

  • Alarms: time, repeat days, the name you type, tone, mission and snooze length.
  • Preferences: numeral style, appearance, night-mode hour, and the bedtime and rest length in Before bed.
  • Wake history: when you stopped or snoozed an alarm or completed its mission.

We receive none of it: the app does not send it anywhere, and we cannot see it. Alarms are scheduled through iOS's own alarm service (AlarmKit) on your device.

If backups are on, this data may be included in your iCloud or computer backup according to your settings; Apple manages those backups, not us.

2. Google ads

The app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (with Google's consent tool, the User Messaging Platform). It is the only part of the app that connects to the internet. According to Google's disclosure page, the SDK may collect:

DataPurpose, as Google states it
IP addressto estimate the device's general location (city or region, not precise location)
Device IDthe device's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, or app- or developer-bounded identifiers — for advertising and analytics
Advertising dataads you have seen, for analytics and advertising features
Product interactionssuch as app launches, taps and ad views, to improve advertising performance
Performance datasuch as launch time and energy use, for analytics and for displaying ads
Crash logsnot related to you, to diagnose problems and improve the SDK

Source: Google, App store data disclosure.

  • Google does not receive your alarms, preferences or history: the app does not give them to it.
  • Google processes this data under its privacy policy, and explains how it uses data from apps that show its ads.
  • Google may process data on servers outside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
  • We share your data with no one other than Google, and we do not sell it. We rely on Google to protect what it processes to a standard no lower than the protection described in this policy, under its published terms and policy.
  • With no internet, with consent declined, or when no ad is available, the app works fully without ads. Alarms never depend on ads.

3. Your choices: tracking and consent

  • Google's consent message: in some regions (such as the EU, the UK and Switzerland) Google shows a consent message on first launch.
  • iOS tracking permission: iOS then asks whether you allow صحصح to track your activity. If you allow it, ads may be personalised. If you do not, every ad stays non-personalised, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not sent, and the app works fully.
  • Change your mind at any time:
    • iPhone Settings → صحصح → Allow Tracking (one switch).
    • or iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
    • and in the app: the «الإعدادات» (Settings) tab → «الإعلانات والموافقة» (Ads and consent), and «إدارة موافقة الإعلانات» (Manage ad consent) where Google's message applies in your region.
  • After you withdraw consent, later ads are non-personalised. Processing done before the withdrawal remains lawful.
  • Version 1.0 has no option to remove ads; non-personalised ads continue on the three screens and after an alarm is ended inside the app.

4. What we do not collect

No name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, voice recordings or health data. The app does not use the microphone or camera, and it does not measure sleep or sleep stages. There are no in-app purchases in version 1.0. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting tool of our own.

If you turn on “Share With App Developers” on your iPhone (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple gives us aggregated crash reports and usage statistics under its own policy. You can turn it off in the same place.

5. Legal basis for processing

Under the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law:

  • Personalised ads: your prior consent (allowing tracking, and Google's message where it applies). You can withdraw it at any time as described in section 3.
  • Non-personalised ads: our legitimate interest in offering the app free of charge, funded by ads, limited to what Google needs to serve and measure the ad and prevent fraud.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it: delete an alarm in its editor, clear the history under «الإعدادات» ← «مسح سجل الصباحات» (Settings → Clear wake history), or delete the app to erase all of its data from the phone.
  • We keep no data about you, so we have nothing to delete.
  • Google's retention is set by its policy.

7. Your rights

The Personal Data Protection Law gives you rights including: to know how and why your data is processed, to access it and obtain a copy, to correct it, to ask for its destruction when it is no longer needed, and to withdraw your consent at any time.

  • Data on your device is in your hands: edit and delete it in the app itself.
  • We hold no data about you, but write to founder@speacetech.com with any request or question and we will reply within 30 days.
  • Data Google processes: stop tracking as in section 3; for requests about your data at Google, see Google's privacy policy.
  • You may lodge a complaint with the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).

8. Children

Sahsah is a general-audience app and is not directed at children; we do not knowingly collect data from anyone. Its ads are set to content suitable for all ages.

9. Security

Your data on your phone is protected by iOS and your device lock. The app does not transmit it, so there is no copy with us that could leak.

10. Terms

Sahsah has no terms of use of its own. Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement applies.

11. Changes to this policy

If what the app collects, or what another party processes, changes, we will update this page and the effective date above before the new version reaches you.
